Meet Shannon

Hi, I'm Shannon! I'm a proud mom of four, a helicopter enthusiast, and the heart behind Got It Maid in the Valley. Over 25 years in industries from beauty and wellness to construction management and experimental aircraft manufacturing taught me the value of hard work, attention to detail, and genuine customer care.

I'm also working toward a master's degree in psychology, and I've seen how much our surroundings affect our well-being. A clean, organized home can ease everyday stress and give you a place to truly exhale.

As a busy mom, I know how precious time is. My goal is to lighten your load, with a team that's proud of what they do, because a happy team makes for happy clients.
