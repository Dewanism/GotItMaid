What to Expect When You're Expecting... Us!

First time having someone clean your home? We've Got It Maid! No need to roll out the red carpet. That's our job... or at least to leave you feeling like we did! A few little things will help us get started:

  • Friends with paws

    We love a friendly welcoming committee! If your pets would rather skip the introductions, please settle them somewhere safe and comfortable so we don't upset them.

  • Let's plan our entrance

    Please arrange access with us before cleaning day, whether that's a door code, a key, or you welcoming us in. Staying home while we clean? We love that too! We promise not to put you to work.

  • Behind-the-scenes cleaning

    Want us to clean behind or around large appliances? Please have them safely pulled out before we arrive. For safety and liability reasons, we can't move heavy appliances ourselves. If they slide back easily and safely, we're happy to return them; otherwise, we'll leave them pulled out for you.

  • A little heads-up goes a long way

    Please tell us about any allergies or sensitivities when booking so we can plan accordingly. We want to be the cure for your headache, not the cause!

You bring the welcome. We'll bring the sparkle.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you bring your own cleaning supplies and equipment?

Yes, we bring everything we need.

Do I need to be home while you clean?

No. We'll never enter your home unless we've arranged it with you beforehand. Life happens, so if you're not there we'll wait 15 minutes and try to reach you before we leave.

Which areas do you serve?

Chilliwack, Hope and Abbotsford. Not sure if we come to you? Send us a message and we'll let you know.

How do you handle payment?

We accept e-transfer, cash or cheque.

Do you clean homes with pets?

Yes! We just ask that pets who spook easily are put away while we work. We definitely don't want to be attacked while vacuuming.

What's your cancellation or rescheduling policy?

If you're on a weekly, biweekly or monthly schedule and you're going away or won't need us, we're very understanding, but we do need at least 72 hours' notice.
